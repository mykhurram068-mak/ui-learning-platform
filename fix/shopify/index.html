Copy-paste fixes for the Shopify problems that interrupt your work. Theme editor, Liquid syntax, and app conflicts — the stuff that doesn't show up in Shopify's own docs.

Why these pages exist: Shopify has great docs for the happy path. But when your theme won't save, Liquid throws a cryptic error, or an app breaks your layout, Shopify's docs point you to their community forum — where the answers are years old. We give you the fix in 30 seconds.

📖 How to use these pages

1
Match the symptom

You don't need to know what's wrong. Find the page that matches what you're seeing — "theme won't save," "Liquid error," "app broke layout."

2
Read the 30-second fix

Every page opens with the fastest safe answer. Copy it, apply it, verify.

3
Test on a duplicate theme

For theme fixes, always work on a duplicate. Shopify lets you duplicate themes in one click — never edit production directly.
