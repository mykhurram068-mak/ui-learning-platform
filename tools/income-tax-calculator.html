📊 FBR Tax Slabs (FY 2025-26) — Salaried Individuals

Your income is taxed in slabs. Each slab has a fixed amount plus a percentage of the amount exceeding the lower limit.

🧠 How the Calculation Works

The calculator uses the official FBR slabs for salaried individuals:

  1. Convert your salary to an annual amount (if entered monthly).
  2. Find the applicable tax slab for your annual income.
  3. Apply the formula: Fixed tax + (Rate × Amount above slab lower limit).
  4. If annual income exceeds Rs. 10 million, add a 10% surcharge.
  5. Divide the annual tax by 12 to get your monthly deduction.

Example: Annual income Rs. 1,800,000.
→ Falls in slab 3 (Rs. 1,200,001–2,200,000)
→ Tax = 6,000 + 11% × (1,800,000 − 1,200,000)
→ Tax = 6,000 + 11% × 600,000 = Rs. 72,000
→ Effective rate = 72,000 ÷ 1,800,000 = 4.0%

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the tax rate for salaried individuals in Pakistan?

For FY 2025-26, salaried individuals pay 0% up to Rs. 600,000, then 1% between Rs. 600,001-1,200,000, and progressively higher rates (11%, 23%, 30%, 35%) for higher income brackets.

Is this calculator accurate for FBR filing?

It uses the official FBR slabs for salaried individuals. However, it's an estimation tool. Always verify with FBR's official calculator or a tax professional before filing.

What is a surcharge and when does it apply?

A 10% surcharge applies if your annual taxable income exceeds Rs. 10 million. This is in addition to the base tax computed from the slabs.

What about tax deductions and exemptions?

This calculator gives gross tax based on salary. Other factors (investment deductions, charitable donations, etc.) can reduce your final liability. Consult FBR rules for these.

Does this apply to non-salaried individuals?

No. Non-salaried individuals (business owners, freelancers) have different tax slabs. This calculator is for salaried persons only.
