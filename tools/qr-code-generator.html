🧠 How QR Codes Work

  1. Pick a type: URL, text, WiFi, email, phone, or SMS.
  2. Enter your data: Click Generate — encoding happens entirely in your browser.
  3. Error correction: Higher levels (Q, H) let the code scan even if part is damaged.
  4. Colors: Keep high contrast (dark on light) for reliable scanning.
  5. Download: Save the PNG for printing or embedding on your website.

Tip: Planning to add a logo? Choose error correction level H.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a QR code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a 2D barcode that stores data — like a URL, text, or contact info. Scanning it with a phone camera opens the linked content instantly.

Do QR codes expire?

No. Static QR codes generated here never expire. They encode the data directly into the pattern.

Do you store the QR codes I create?

No. All QR codes are generated locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.
