❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert cm to inches?

Divide the number of centimeters by 2.54. For example, 10 cm = 10 ÷ 2.54 = 3.94 inches. Use the Length converter above.

How do I convert kg to lbs?

Multiply the number of kilograms by 2.20462. For example, 5 kg = 5 × 2.20462 = 11.02 lbs. Use the Weight converter above.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9/5 and then add 32. For example, 25°C = (25 × 9/5) + 32 = 77°F. Use the Temperature converter above.

Is this unit converter accurate?

Yes. All conversions use standard, internationally-recognized conversion factors. The tool is designed for everyday use, schoolwork, and quick reference.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. All conversions run locally in your browser. No numbers are sent anywhere. No tracking, no ads.
