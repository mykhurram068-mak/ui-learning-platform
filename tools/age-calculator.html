🧠 How Age is Calculated

  1. Your date of birth is subtracted from the target date (today by default).
  2. The result is broken down into years, months, and days — accounting for leap years and month length variations.
  3. Total days, hours, minutes, and seconds are also calculated.
  4. Fun stats use approximate averages (75 heartbeats/min, 8 hours sleep/night, 16 breaths/min, 3 meals/day).

Leap year handling: The calculator counts Feb 29 correctly. If you were born on Feb 29, your birthday is treated as Feb 28 in non-leap years for the countdown.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated exactly?

Age is calculated by subtracting your date of birth from today's date. The result is broken down into years, months, and days — accounting for leap years and varying month lengths.

How many days have I been alive?

Enter your date of birth above and the calculator shows your total days lived, along with hours, minutes, and other life stats.

Why does my age differ by a day between calculators?

Time zone differences and how calculators handle the day of birth can cause off-by-one results. This calculator uses your local time zone and includes today in the count if calculating as of today.

What is the difference between chronological and biological age?

Chronological age is time since you were born (what this calculator shows). Biological age reflects how old your body appears based on health markers — it can differ from chronological age.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser. No date of birth is sent anywhere. No tracking, no ads.
