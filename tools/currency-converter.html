❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How often are rates updated?

Rates are fetched from the currency-api via jsDelivr, which updates daily. Values are cached for 24 hours to keep the tool fast.

Are these rates accurate for real transactions?

These are mid-market rates (interbank rates). Banks add a 1-3% margin. Use this as an estimate, not the exact amount your bank will charge.

Which currencies are supported?

Over 200 currencies including PKR, USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, INR, CAD, AUD, MYR, JPY, CNY, and most major world currencies.

Why is the rate slightly different from Google?

Google shows live mid-market rates that change in real-time. We cache for 24 hours to avoid hitting API rate limits. For most use cases, the difference is under 0.5%.

Is this tool free?

Yes, completely free. No signup, no ads, no limits. The tool runs entirely in your browser.
